How to convert Google Docs or Word content to HTML

  1. Copy your formatted content and paste it into Input Content, or upload a .txt or .html file.
  2. Choose your conversion options. Select Simple HTML for output without layout wrappers or classes.
  3. Select Convert and review the result in Quick Preview.
  4. Copy the HTML into your blog editor or download it as an HTML file.

The converter processes content locally in your browser. It keeps supported text formatting and links while removing source images and pasted styling.

Blog wrapper mode maps H1–H2 to H2 and H3–H6 to H3. Simple HTML mode preserves heading levels. When using image or Shop Now placeholders, add your image URLs, descriptive alt text and destination links before publishing.
